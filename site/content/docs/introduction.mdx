Your agent makes many small decisions on every run. Which tools does the model need for this
task? Is this tool call safe to run? Today the big model decides by default, and you pay for it
in tokens.

krino puts a small, cheap decision model next to your agent. It asks that model the same small
questions, records the answers, and tells you what you would save by trusting them.

## What krino does

- **Watches.** In shadow mode, krino asks the decision model in the background and records its
  answers. Your agent behaves exactly as before and waits for nothing.
- **Prices.** `npx krino report` reads the traces and shows, per decision, how often krino
  agreed with your agent and what it would save. Costs include prompt-cache reads and writes.
- **Cheapens, safely.** When the data agrees, turn on enforce mode for tool selection. krino then
  sends only the tools the task needs. If the decision model is slow or unsure, your agent gets
  all its tools.

## The two decisions in v0.1

| Decision | The question | When unsure |
|---|---|---|
| Tool selection | Which of the agent's tools does this task need? | Send all tools (fails open) |
| Risk gate | Is this tool call safe to run without asking a person? | Suggest asking a human (fails closed) |

The risk gate runs in shadow mode only in v0.1: it records what it would do and never blocks a
call.

## Who it is for

krino is for TypeScript agents built on the **Vercel AI SDK** or the **Claude Agent SDK**,
especially agents with many tools, where tool definitions are a large part of every prompt.

## What krino is not

- **Not a proxy.** krino is a library that runs inside your process. Nothing sits between you
  and your model provider.
- **Not a model router.** Your agent keeps its model. krino only answers small questions about
  tools.
- **Not a hosted service.** Traces are files on your machine.
